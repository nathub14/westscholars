Everyone says "we get results." Here's what we actually do differently.

Most tutoring is a worksheet and a red pen. This is a platform, a method, and a person who has personally sat every section of the exam he now teaches.

💻

A real practice platform, not photocopies

Students work through a custom-built online system that assigns questions by skill, tracks every attempt, and resurfaces weak spots with spaced repetition — the same kind of tech Alpha School-style programs use, purpose-built for ASET and scholarship exams.

Writing marked every single week

CIIW writing is the section most tutors skip because it's slow to mark. Every student's writing is marked weekly, with specific, actionable feedback — not a vague "great job."

Homework built from real diagnostics

No generic take-home packs. Homework is assigned from each student's actual mock results and platform data — the questions they got wrong, the skills they're shakiest on.

Definition flashcards and named "failure modes" for common traps mean students learn to recognise a trick, not just memorise a topic.

Parents see the data, not just a report card

Mock results, per-skill progress, and what's being targeted next are shared with parents directly — you always know exactly where your child stands.
