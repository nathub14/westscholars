How it works

The Zero Gaps Scientific Scholar System

We use a four step system that means nothing your child gets wrong stays wrong. It runs every week of the term.

1

We teach the skill in the Workshop

The Workshop is two hours with four students in the room, in person. We teach the new skill and demonstrate it, then the group works through it with help before each student does it on their own.

2

Your child practises it that week

Through the week your child works through a mix of paper and platform homework, built from the questions they actually got wrong. Every question on the platform carries a video solution, so a student stuck at eight on a Tuesday night is not stuck for long.

3

We go through it one-on-one in the Clinic

The Clinic is 45 minutes face to face, every week of the term. We go through what your child got wrong, teach it again, and have them redo it while they can still remember what they were thinking the first time.

4

We patch the holes that are left

A diagnostic finds the narrow slice of the syllabus costing your child the most marks, and the next week's homework aims at that and nothing else.

The diagnostic and the homework loop run on spaced repetition and deliberate practice, two of the best evidenced findings in learning research. Ask us and we will walk you through how we apply both.

Before you enquire

Questions parents ask before they enrol

What is ASET, GATE, or a scholarship exam?
ASET is the exam that decides entry into WA's selective public programs, known as GATE, including Perth Modern. Private schools run their own scholarship exams, worth up to 100% off six years of fees, which is around $180,000 at a full fee school. Almost all of them are sat at the start of Year 6, and applications close earlier than most parents expect. If any of this is new, grab the free guide or send us a message and we will walk you through the whole system.
What year level is this for?
Mostly Year 4 and Year 5, because that is the window for the Year 7 entry exams sat at the start of Year 6. Other year levels are one of the areas we are building out, so if your child sits outside that window it is worth asking.
Is Year 5 too early to start?
The tests are sat at the start of Year 6, and private school application lists close more than a year before that. By the middle of Year 5 you are already inside the window.
My child is not sitting these exams. Can you still help?
Very likely, yes. NAPLAN, getting a year or more ahead, competition maths like AMC and Olympiad, ATAR subjects and uni-ready work all sit inside what this system handles. None of them run as a scheduled class yet, so we would design it with you. Tell us what your child needs in the enquiry form and you will get a straight answer either way.
Who teaches it?
Nathan Hubbard, Senior Tutor, teaches every Workshop and every Clinic. He sat these exams, won an academic scholarship to Hale, finished with a 98 ATAR and studies Finance and Computer Science at UWA. Anyone who teaches here will have sat and won these exams themselves.
What makes it science-based?
Two things in particular. Spaced repetition, which means content your child has learned resurfaces at widening intervals instead of being covered once and forgotten. And deliberate practice, which means the work is aimed narrowly at what they currently cannot do, at a difficulty just above their level, with fast feedback. Both are among the most heavily evidenced findings in learning research, and both are hard enough to run properly that most tutoring skips them.
Why not just go to a big tutoring centre?
At a large centre your child is one of twenty doing the same worksheet, and the writing section tends to get skipped because it is slow to mark. Here the class is four kids and there is a one-on-one Clinic every week, with homework built from your child's own mistakes. The full comparison is above.
Where are classes held, and when?
In person in Perth, at a public library, with both weekday and weekend sessions. We confirm the venue and the timetable with each intake before the term starts.
What happens if it does not work for us?
The Sleep Like a Baby Guarantee. If you want your money back you can get it, at any point in the term.
How much homework is there?
Short and daily, plus one writing piece a week. Everything assigned comes from something your child got wrong.
Is there a sibling or referral discount?
Yes. Current families get $100 cash back for every family they bring on board, siblings included, and the family they refer gets $100 back too. There is no cap on how many families you refer.
