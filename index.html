How it works

The Zero Gaps Scientific Scholar System

Four steps, on repeat, every week of the term. Nothing your child gets wrong is allowed to stay wrong.

1

We teach the skill in the Workshop

A two hour group class, four kids, in person. New skills get taught and demonstrated, and the questions the whole room got wrong go on the whiteboard.

2

Your child practises it that week

Homework made from your child's real mistakes and the things they are struggling with, on our practice platform, with video explanation solutions on every question.

3

The Clinic reviews what they got wrong

45 minutes, one-on-one, every week. Face to face, going through their own work with them, which is where the real feedback happens.

4

We patch the holes that are left

We use a science-backed diagnostic to pinpoint the exact 1% of the syllabus dragging your child's score down, and patch it instantly with homework aimed at nothing else.

The science in question is not decoration. The diagnostic and the homework loop are built on spaced repetition and deliberate practice, two of the most heavily evidenced findings in learning research, and we are happy to talk any parent through exactly how we apply them.

Commonly asked questions

What is ASET, GATE, or a scholarship exam?
ASET is the exam that decides entry into WA's selective public programs, known as GATE, including Perth Modern. Private schools run their own scholarship exams, worth up to 100% off six years of fees. Almost all of them are sat at the start of Year 6, and applications close earlier than most parents expect. If any of this is new, grab the free guide or message me and I will walk you through the whole system.
What year level is this for?
Mostly Year 4 and Year 5, because that is the window for the Year 7 entry exams sat at the start of Year 6. We are open to other year levels where there is enough demand, so it is worth asking.
Is Year 5 too early to start?
The tests are sat at the start of Year 6, and private school application lists close more than a year ahead. Year 5 is not early. It is the window.
Who actually teaches it?
I do. I am a tutor, and right now I teach every class myself. I sat these exact exams, won an academic scholarship to Hale, got a 98 ATAR, and I am studying Finance and Computer Science. Anyone who ever teaches here will have personally sat and won these exams, because nobody who has only read about them can teach the parts that matter.
What makes it science-based?
Two specific things, not a general claim. Spaced repetition, which means content your child has learned resurfaces at widening intervals instead of being covered once and forgotten. And deliberate practice, which means the work is aimed narrowly at what they currently cannot do, at a difficulty just above their level, with fast feedback. Both are among the most heavily evidenced findings in learning research, and both are hard enough to run properly that most tutoring skips them.
Why not just go to a big tutoring centre?
At a large centre your child is one of twenty doing the same worksheet, and the writing section usually gets skipped because it is slow to mark. Here the class is four kids, there is a one-on-one Clinic every week, homework is made from your child's actual mistakes, and you have a real number to call. See the full comparison above.
Where are classes held, and when?
In person in Perth, with both weekday and weekend sessions available. Message us for the current location and times.
What happens if it does not work for us?
The Sleep Like a Baby Guarantee. You can get all your money back, at any point in the term, for any reason. No conditions and nothing to prove. If it was not worth it for your family, you get a full refund.
How much homework is there?
There is a floor, not a ceiling, and your child sets their own commitment above it each week. In practice it is a short daily set plus a weekly writing piece. The point is consistency, not volume, and everything assigned is targeted at something they specifically got wrong.
