Quick written review
Prefer to type it here instead? Form coming soon.

Not sure what to write? Steal one of these.

Pick whichever is true for you, tweak it, done. Honest and specific beats long and generic.

What changed for your child this term?
Think confidence, specific skills, or exam habits — not just "he improved."
"My daughter went from freezing up on writing prompts to actually planning her ideas before she starts — that alone was worth it."
What's different about this compared to other tutoring you've tried?
The platform, the weekly writing marking, the personalised homework — whatever stood out to you.
"Every week's homework was actually built around what my son got wrong the week before, not a generic worksheet everyone gets."
Would you recommend West Scholars to another parent, and why?
This is the line that convinces the next family — be specific about who it's a good fit for.
"If your child is sitting ASET or a scholarship exam next year, I'd tell you to get in now — classes are small and it shows."
