Ask yourself these three as you write

A review that answers all three is worth more than a long one that answers none. Honest and specific beats polished.

What changed for your child this term?
Confidence, specific skills, exam habits, rather than just that they improved.
What is different about this compared to other tutoring you have tried?
The group class and the one-on-one, the constant feedback, the test techniques.
Would you recommend West Scholars to another parent, and why?
Be specific about who it is a good fit for. This is the line that convinces the next family.
